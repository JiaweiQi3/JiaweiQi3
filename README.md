### Hi, I'm Jiawei Qi

I'm an M.S. student in Civil and Environmental Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University.
I study how the growth of AI data centers reshapes electricity demand and grid planning,
using machine learning, data pipelines, and energy-system modeling.

**Research interests:** data center load forecasting | energy systems modeling | artificial intelligence and machine learning | power markets and policy

**Selected projects**
- [U.S. data center electricity demand forecasts](https://github.com/JiaweiQi3/us-datacenter-electricity-forecasts)
  - Harmonized 47 forecast curves from 16 institutions in a MySQL database
  - 2030 U.S. data center demand forecasts range from 424 to 925 TWh
  - The largest gap between institutions grows from 313 TWh in 2028 to 501 TWh in 2030
- [Behind-the-meter battery as a capacity service for PJM data centers](https://github.com/JiaweiQi3/datacenter-battery-capacity-service) (work in progress)
  - Feasibility study and 15-year project-finance model for a 50 MW / 500 MWh battery
  - Minimum fee of $27.65/kW-month for a 12% equity return
  - Being redesigned after FERC rejected the underlying PJM rule in October 2026
- [Residential energy burden and assistance access in Los Angeles County](https://github.com/JiaweiQi3/la-county-energy-burden-gis)
  - Census-tract GIS analysis of 2,437 tracts in ArcGIS Pro and Python
  - Getis-Ord Gi* hot spots of energy burden and distance to HEAP/LIHEAP centers
  - The northern county is the one area that is both high-burden and far from assistance
- [Building characteristics and energy use in New York City](https://github.com/JiaweiQi3/nyc-building-energy-drivers)
  - Regression study of 50,635 Local Law 84 benchmarking records in a MySQL database
  - Floor area alone explains about one-third of the variance in total site energy use
  - Building age has an inverted-U effect, with energy use peaking at about 80 years
